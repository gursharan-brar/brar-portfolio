About me

More than just
an IT guy.

Before I ever touched a server, I spent years in customer-facing roles — Best Buy Canada, 7-Eleven, Reliance Smart — learning to listen, stay calm under pressure, and explain complicated things to people who just want their problem solved. That shapes how I approach IT: I care about the person on the other end, not just the ticket.

I graduated from SAIT's IT Services Diploma (SADT stream). I was Group Lead + AI Lead on CareFlow AI — final showcase 29/30 — founded Northline IT to serve Calgary SMBs, and picked up a Best Design Award at the PMC Product Sprint Hackathon. I'm fluent in Punjabi and English, and I always make sure everyone's on the same page before we ship.

My industry mentor Bonnie Nasim put it well when she said I "consistently demonstrate outstanding values, compassion, and kindness" — and honestly, that matters in IT just as much as knowing your way around a VLAN. I volunteer at the Calgary Drop-In Centre and serve at my local Gurdwara because service is something I was raised with.
